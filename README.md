## Project Description

**Useless Evolution Simulator** is an interactive simulation where virtual creatures live, move, reproduce, mutate, and evolve over generations.

The project creates a digital ecosystem and lets its creatures change over time through genetics, mutation, reproduction, environmental pressure, and survival.

It doesn't solve a real-world problem.

It exists simply because watching tiny digital creatures evolve seemed like a good way to spend an unreasonable amount of time.

---

## The Problem (that doesn't exist)

What if we created a world full of creatures and simply let them evolve?

Would they survive?

Would they reproduce?

Would new species appear?

Would some species disappear?

Would anything useful come out of it?

Probably not.

But we still wanted to find out.

---

## The Solution (that nobody asked for)

We built a virtual ecosystem where creatures can live and evolve through multiple generations.

The simulation allows creatures to:

- Move around the environment
- Interact with their surroundings
- Reproduce
- Pass traits to their offspring
- Experience genetic mutations
- Develop different characteristics
- Compete for survival
- Adapt to environmental conditions
- Form different populations and species
- Become extinct

The simulation continues running as generations pass, allowing the ecosystem to change without requiring the user to manually control every creature.
